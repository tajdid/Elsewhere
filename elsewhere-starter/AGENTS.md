# Elsewhere project instructions
Read README.md and docs/01-PRD.md through docs/06-IMPLEMENTATION-CODEX.md before extending this starter.

- Keep Astro static output, TypeScript strict and Tailwind 4 via @tailwindcss/vite. Use native HTML and small client TypeScript; do not add React or a backend without product need.
- Preserve explicit Elsewhere brand, Geist and tokens. Use design-taste-frontend for design work when installed; read the applicable skill first. User requirements override skill defaults. Record intentional deviations and review real screenshots at phone/tablet/desktop sizes.
- Never invent factual claims, prices, accessibility, Wi-Fi speeds, opening status, reviews or provenance about real venues. On 27 Nov 2026 the owner hid the 53 places imported from the Notion list “WFH in Dubai” (13 Nov 2026): they are parked in src/data/hidden-places.json (with their Google place IDs), which nothing under src/ imports, so they are neither built nor shipped. Move an entry back to src/data/places.json to list it again; never re-run scripts/notion-to-places.py over places.json. The site lists 15 places, all from the Submit a place Sheet once the owner published them. Four are visitor reports, added 26–27 Nov 2026 (Café De Paris, Counter Culture Café, Splendour Fields, Single Fin Cafe): their answers are Reported with source “Visitor report”, dated to the report. Eleven were entered by the owner on 27 Nov 2026 from a community recommendation and an Instagram find: their answers are Reported with source “Community recommendation” (or “Instagram”), dated 27 Nov 2026, and their street locations are kept as location notes, not validated addresses. Unanswered questions stay unknown. Every fact is copied from that list and dated by when the entry was added (2020–2023): status is Reported, never Verified, until someone re-checks it. Missing facts stay null/unknown and the UI shows “not added” placeholders; never fill them from general knowledge about similarly named businesses. Areas are set only where the list names them. At the owner’s request, guest Wi-Fi passwords from the list are shown with a Copy button, labelled with when they were listed. At the owner’s request, places show no photos: listings and place pages are described by their details alone, and About’s “Good to know” says there are no photos yet. Do not add stock or placeholder imagery of venues. Only add a photo of a venue once there is a licensed photo of that place, with its provenance, caption and licence. The owner’s own photographs of working around Dubai (public/images/photos, taken by Tajdid Rahman, metadata stripped) appear editorially only, in the About page photo strip under its header (provenance in docs/SOURCES.md); never caption them as, or attach them to, a particular listed venue. The old stock images in public/images are no longer used by the site. Directions use the owner’s Google Maps link when present, otherwise a labelled Google Maps search by name. The owner launched on 19 Nov 2026: Vercel Production builds are indexable (switch off with PUBLIC_ALLOW_INDEXING=false); previews and local builds stay noindex.
- Verification is per amenity. Reported, unknown, conflicting, stale and no never pass a verified-positive filter. Keep verification source/date/context. Do not bump dates to hide staleness.
- Do not add a global verified venue badge or infer access from photos. Production is indexable since launch (19 Nov 2026); keep Saved, Submit, 404 and thin place pages noindex, and previews/local builds noindex. Preserve local-only save behavior and failure feedback.
- URLs are the Explore state. Keep keyboard semantics, native controls, live announcements and visible focus. No clickable divs or nested interactive controls.
- Run npm test and npm run build after meaningful changes. Run browser tests for interaction changes. Do not claim tests or accessibility checks ran unless executed. Review image crops, 320px overflow and 200% zoom for layout work.
- Maintain docs and tests when contracts change. No secrets in client code; no unsolicited analytics/auth/payments. Do not buy domain, deploy or send venue outreach unless requested.
- Ship with a lockfile; exclude node_modules, .astro, dist, test-results and playwright-report from source ZIPs. Preserve media credit/license information.
